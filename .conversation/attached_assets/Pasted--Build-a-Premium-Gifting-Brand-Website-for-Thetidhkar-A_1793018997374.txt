Required:
- Smooth reveal-on-scroll for major sections.
- Gentle stagger for product cards.
- Soft hero entrance animation.
- Image hover zoom.
- Button hover and tap feedback.
- Smooth mobile menu transition.
- FAQ accordion animation.
- Product modal entrance and exit.
- Sticky navbar transition.

Animation guidelines:
- Short durations, approximately 0.25–0.7 seconds for most interactions.
- Soft easing curves.
- Avoid excessive bouncing, dramatic rotation, or constant movement.
- Use viewport-based triggers so offscreen sections animate as they appear.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Avoid layout shifts and performance-heavy animation.
- The site must remain usable when animation is disabled.
